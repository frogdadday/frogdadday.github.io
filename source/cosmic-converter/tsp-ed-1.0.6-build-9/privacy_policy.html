Privacy Policy for Cosmic Converter

Effective date: September 10, 2026

The short version: Cosmic Converter does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information or sensitive user data. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or user tracking.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Cosmic Converter Android application (the “App”), provided by Frogdadday (the “Developer”).

Information the App collects

Cosmic Converter does not collect, store, or transmit personal information or sensitive user data. The App does not require an account and does not request a user's name, email address, location, contacts, photos, files, advertising identifier, or other identifying information.

Values entered for unit conversions are processed locally on the user's device. They are not transmitted to the Developer or saved on a Developer-operated server.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The App contains no advertisements and uses no advertising, analytics, profiling, or tracking services. The Developer does not sell or share App user data because the App does not collect such data.

Device permissions

Cosmic Converter does not request sensitive or user-facing Android permissions. Its conversion features operate locally on the device.

Optional external support link

The App includes an optional “Send Me a Coffee” button. Selecting it opens the Developer's Buy Me a Coffee page in an external browser or application. Support is never required, does not unlock features, and does not affect access to the App.

Once a user leaves Cosmic Converter, information voluntarily provided to Buy Me a Coffee is handled by that service under its own privacy policy and terms. Cosmic Converter does not track whether a user visits or uses that external service.

Google Play and device services

Google Play and the user's device or operating-system provider may independently process information related to app distribution, installation, security, or device operation. That processing is governed by those providers' own privacy policies and is not data collection by Cosmic Converter.

Children's privacy

Cosmic Converter does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Data retention and deletion

Because the App does not collect or store personal information, the Developer has no App user data to retain or delete.

Direct communications

If a user voluntarily contacts the Developer by email, the Developer will receive the email address and information included in that message. This information is used only to read and respond to the inquiry, is not sold, and is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to address the communication or meet applicable legal obligations.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if Cosmic Converter's features or data practices change. Any revised policy will be posted at this same public address with an updated effective date.
