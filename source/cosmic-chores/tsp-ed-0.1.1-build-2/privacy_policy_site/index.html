Cosmic Chores Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 3, 2026

Our promise

Free. Simple. Respectful. Cosmic Chores has no ads, subscriptions, paid features, accounts, analytics, or tracking. Frogdadday does not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal information.

Information kept on your device

Cosmic Chores stores the information you enter so your chore lists remain available between app sessions. This can include:

  • chore-list names and schedule choices;
  • people's names and, when you choose to add them, phone numbers;
  • chores and daily assignments;
  • daily mission-completion checkmarks;
  • rotation choices; and
  • reminder times.

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Cosmic Chores does not have a developer server and does not upload this information to Frogdadday. Your Android device or backup provider may handle device backups according to your device settings and that provider's privacy terms.

Optional contact access

You can always enter people manually. If you choose Search contacts, Cosmic Chores first explains why contact access is requested and then asks for Android's Contacts permission.

With permission, the app searches contact names and phone numbers on your device as you type. Search results are processed locally. Only a person you select is added to the chore list. Cosmic Chores does not upload your address book or send contact information to Frogdadday.

You can deny or later revoke contact access in Android Settings. Manual entry remains available without this permission.

Notifications and reminders

If you enable a scheduled reminder, Cosmic Chores asks for notification permission on Android versions that require it. The app stores the reminder schedule on your device and uses Android's local scheduling and restart services to restore reminders after a device restart. These services do not send your chore information to Frogdadday.

You can deny or revoke notification access in Android Settings. Your saved lists will still work without notifications.

Messages you choose to send

Cosmic Chores does not silently or automatically send text messages. When you choose to share a day's chores, the app opens an installed messaging or sharing app with a prepared message and any phone numbers you previously added. You review the message and decide whether to send it.

Once you continue in another app, that app, your mobile carrier, and the message recipients handle the information under their own privacy practices.

External links

The optional Send Me a Coffee button opens Buy Me a Coffee in your browser. Support is never required and never changes access to app features. Buy Me a Coffee and your browser have their own privacy practices.

The Privacy button opens this public policy in your browser.

Data sharing, retention, and deletion

Frogdadday does not receive or share your chore-list information. Information is shared outside Cosmic Chores only when you deliberately open a messaging or sharing app to send it.

Saved list information remains on your device until you edit or delete the list, clear Cosmic Chores' app data, or uninstall the app. Frogdadday cannot view, recover, or delete information stored on your device because Frogdadday never receives it. Copies created by Android backups, messaging apps, carriers, or recipients are controlled by those services and people.

Security

Cosmic Chores relies on Android's app-private storage and permission controls. No method of storing information is risk-free, so keep your device protected and install operating-system security updates when available.

Children

Cosmic Chores is a general-audience household tool. It is not directed to children under 13, and Frogdadday does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If Cosmic Chores' data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version of the app is released. The effective date above will also be updated.

Privacy questions

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the Frogdadday GitHub profile or open an issue in the public Cosmic Chores Privacy repository.

No catch. Just a free app made to be useful.
