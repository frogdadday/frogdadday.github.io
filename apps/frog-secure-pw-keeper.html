Local security, explained plainly.

  • Encrypted local vaultVault contents use AES-256-GCM encryption with additional protection from Android Keystore.
  • No account or network accessThe app has no internet permission, online account, WebView, analytics, advertising, or network code.
  • Protective defaultsPasswords stay masked, the app locks when backgrounded, secure windows limit screenshots, and copied values are cleared on a best-effort timer.
  • Master-password-encrypted backupManual encrypted .gpv export and import use Android’s system file picker. Each backup requires the master password used when it was exported. Import requires confirmation and replaces the entire vault instead of merging entries.
  • Built-in password generationCreate local 16-, 20-, 24-, or 32-character passwords, with confirmation before replacing an existing field.
  • Developer self-assessment against OWASP MASVS/MASTGThe developer maintains an OWASP MASVS/MASTG-based checklist. This is a self-assessment, not an independent audit or certification.

Switching phones? Export an encrypted .gpv backup from the old phone before switching or resetting it. Transfer that file to the new phone, then import it using the master password from the time of export. Android automatic backup and cloud sync will not move the vault for you. This version has no master-password change feature; older backups still require their original password.

Autofill: Frog Secure PW Keeper does not offer Android Autofill or run an accessibility service. Copying credentials is a manual action.

Why this one? A deliberately offline vault without an account or recovery server.

The gallery is intentionally empty

No screenshots. By design.

Frog Secure PW Keeper uses Android secure-window protections to restrict screenshots and screen recordings. I am not disabling that protection just to take promotional pictures of a password vault.

Encryption and key derivationThe master password is processed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (600,000 iterations, randomly generated 128-bit salt) to derive a 256-bit key. Vault data uses AES-256-GCM with Android Keystore protection. The master password is not stored.
Local and offlineNo internet permission, account, cloud sync, ads or tracking. Your vault stays on your device unless you explicitly export an encrypted backup or copy something elsewhere.
20-second clipboard protectionCopied passwords are marked sensitive, and the app attempts to clear its own clipboard entry after 20 seconds. Android or other apps can delay or retain copies, so clearing is best-effort.

Screenshot restrictions reduce accidental exposure; they cannot stop an external camera or a compromised device. There is no master-password recovery service, so keep your password and any encrypted backup safe.

Privacy & permissions

The app has no internet permission, Autofill service, or accessibility service. Your encrypted vault stays on your device unless you manually export an encrypted backup. A manual backup is the only supported way to move the vault to another phone; the policy explains restoration and deletion.
