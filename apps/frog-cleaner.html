Optional special access, explained

  • All-files access (Android 11+): An optional setting for scanning more of shared storage to find large or old files. You can use Android’s folder picker instead. Some Android-protected folders remain off-limits even with all-files access.
  • Accessibility-assisted cache cleaning: An optional service for a cleaning session you start. It can open an app’s Android Settings screen and use the visible Clear cache control on supported devices. It is not intended to press Clear data or Clear storage; unknown screens should be skipped. You can stop the session.
  • Your choice: Neither special access is required for basic selected-folder reviews. Grant access only if you understand what it allows, and revoke it through Android Settings whenever you want.

These permissions have additional Google Play policy requirements; a Play listing should not be treated as approved until reviewed there.
