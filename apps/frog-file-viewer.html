Frog File Viewer

View and edit PDFs, work with images, and convert selected documents—without sending your files to a developer server.

← Back to all apps

Your files. Your phone. Your call.

  • PDF viewing and editingOpen PDFs, annotate them, manage pages, split documents, and save copies without overwriting originals.
  • Pictures into PDFsCombine selected images into a PDF, or convert individual images among PNG, JPG, and WebP.
  • Selected document conversionsConvert DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, ODT, or TXT to PDF locally, with limits and possible formatting changes.
  • Text from searchable PDFsExport PDF text to TXT, basic DOCX, or ODT. Scans without text need OCR, which is not currently included.
  • Local edit recoveryAn on-device temporary recovery copy can help protect unsaved edits from interruptions.
  • Always freeNo subscriptions, ads, account requirement, or developer-side document uploads are planned.

Work in progress, not release-verified.

Features listed above exist in source, but real-device testing, document-fidelity checks, signed release validation, and a finalized privacy policy are still outstanding. Conversions may lose layout, graphics, fonts, formulas, or other formatting. This app should not be relied on for critical documents until properly validated.

Installing an APK on Android

  1. Once available, download the APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open the file. Android may ask permission to install apps from your browser or file manager.
  3. Review system warnings; proceed only when you trust the source. Turn off the extra installation permission afterward.

Website APKs do not receive Google Play automatic updates. Updating an installed app requires a compatible Android package and signing certificate.

This page checks whether a public APK exists; it does not independently verify the file's safety or signing certificate.
