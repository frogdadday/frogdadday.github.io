Frog Construction Toolkit
Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Frog Construction Toolkit is a free construction planning and materials-list app. Your job information stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

Information the app stores

The app stores the jobs, projects, measurements, material lists, checklists, notes, and other planning information you enter on your device. This information is used only to provide the app’s planning features.

Information the app does not collect

Frog Construction Toolkit does not collect, sell, rent, transmit, or share your personal information or job information with us or third parties. It does not use advertising, subscriptions, paywalls, analytics, tracking, or an account system.

Backups and sharing

You can choose to export or share a job packet or backup. When you use those features, you decide where the exported information goes, such as a file, text message, email, or another app. The privacy practices of the destination you select apply once you share it.

Internet and links

The app can open external web links when you choose to use resources such as building-code references or support links. Those websites have their own privacy policies. The app does not send your job information to those websites.

Permissions

Frog Construction Toolkit is designed to work without unnecessary permissions. If a future version requests a device permission, it will explain what the permission is used for before you choose whether to allow it.

Children

The app is a general construction planning tool and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the public Frog Construction Toolkit privacy-policy repository. Please do not post sensitive information in a public issue.
