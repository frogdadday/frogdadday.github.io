Cosmic Spinner

Privacy Policy & Responsible Use

Effective date: September 13, 2026

This page applies to the Cosmic Spinner Android app, provided by Frogdadday (the Developer).

Privacy policyResponsible use & disclaimerContact

Free. Simple. Respectful. Cosmic Spinner works with choices you enter on your device. The app does not send your wheels or drafts to the Developer. No accounts, ads, subscriptions, paid features, analytics SDKs, or user tracking. Optional support never unlocks features or affects access.

Privacy policy

Information stored and used on your device

The app stores your saved spinner titles, slice text, color choices, unfinished draft, and preferences such as sound and cosmic mode in its private Android app storage. Text you enter may contain personal information if you choose to include it. This information is used locally to edit, save, display, and spin your wheels.

The Developer does not receive this content through the app, operate an account or cloud-sync service for it, or sell it. The app does not require your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or advertising identifier.

Local keyword support message

When you explicitly save a spinner, the app checks its title and choices against a local keyword list. A match may display a dismissible support message with the U.S. 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. The check does not use internet AI, send your text to a server, contact anyone, or create a report for the Developer. The spinner is still saved. The message is not triggered just by opening or spinning a saved wheel; an edited save may trigger it again.

Advertising, tracking, and permissions

The app contains no advertising, analytics, profiling, or third-party tracking SDKs. Its core features work locally without an internet connection and do not request sensitive Android runtime permissions. Sound effects may use your device's text-to-speech service for the fixed phrase “Hi-ya!”; your spinner text is not sent to that service.

Retention, deletion, and backups

Saved wheels remain until you delete them or clear the app's data. A draft may be updated automatically while editing; you can discard it. You can delete saved wheels in the app, or clear the app's storage through Android settings to remove local wheels, drafts, and preferences. Uninstalling normally removes local app data.

Android or your device provider may include app data in backups or device transfers depending on your device and settings. Backed-up data may be restored after reinstalling. Those backups are controlled by your device or account provider, not the Developer; manage or delete them using that provider's settings. The Developer cannot access or remotely delete your local wheels or those backups.

The app uses Android's private app storage. No storage system can guarantee absolute security; avoid putting information in a wheel that you do not want others with access to your device to see.

Optional support and external services

The Support button opens the Developer's Buy Me a Coffee page in your browser or another app. Support is entirely optional. Cosmic Spinner does not send your spinner content with that link or track whether you donate.

External services may process information such as your IP address, browser details, cookies, and information you choose to provide under their own policies. If you donate, the Developer may receive supporter information made available by that service, such as a name or message; payment processing is handled by the external service. See Buy Me a Coffee's privacy policy.

This policy webpage is hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting it is separate from using the app; GitHub may process connection and usage information under GitHub's privacy statement. This page contains no added analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

Google Play, your operating system, backup provider, and selected text-to-speech provider may independently process information related to their services under their own policies.

Children and direct communications

The app does not transmit personal information to the Developer from any user, including children. There are no in-app accounts, chat, or public sharing features.

If you email the Developer, the Developer receives your address and what you include in the message. That information is used to address your inquiry, is not sold, and is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. Please do not send sensitive spinner content that is not needed to explain your question.

No intent assessment or emergency monitoring

Neither a warning nor the absence of one is a safety assessment. The app is not a crisis service and does not monitor users or summon help.

In the U.S., call or text 988 for suicide or mental-health crisis support. Call 911 for an immediate life-threatening emergency. Displaying these numbers does not connect you automatically.
