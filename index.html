Fair questions

What’s the catch?

Are the apps really free?

Yes. I don’t charge for features, use subscriptions, run ads, or lock useful tools behind payments.

Where can I download them?

Open an app’s detail page to check for a direct APK. Download buttons appear when GitHub currently lists the official APK release. Android may ask permission to install from your browser. Website APKs do not receive Google Play automatic updates; Play links will be added separately when available.

What about my data?

The apps do not collect personal data unless a feature clearly tells you otherwise. The public Frog Pond stores what is needed to display and protect comments, and explains that before you post.
